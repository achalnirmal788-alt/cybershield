Learn About Phishing

Understand what phishing is, how the 5-stage attack lifecycle works, and how to defend against all 6 major types.

What is Phishing?

Phishing is a form of social engineering where attackers deceive individuals into revealing confidential credentials (passwords, banking OTPs, debit card numbers, identity files) by posing as trusted organizations such as national banks, postal services, utility companies, or friends.

How Phishing Works (5 Attack Stages)

01. RECON

Target Selection

Harvesting emails & phone numbers from data breaches and social media.

02. WEAPONIZE

Crafting Bait

Cloning legitimate logos, forms, and panic notices.

03. DELIVER

Lure Dispatch

Blasting fraudulent SMS, emails, WhatsApp messages, or QR codes.

04. DECEIVE

Credential Theft

Victim submits username, password, OTP, or PIN into the fake form.

05. EXPLOIT

Account Draining

Fraudsters instantly transfer savings or hijack social profiles.

The 6 Primary Phishing Types

1. Email Phishing Critical

Fraudulent emails mimicking trusted companies to steal login credentials, reset tokens, or push malicious attachments.

Clue: Look for slight typos in sender domain (@apple-security.co instead of @apple.com).
2. SMS / Smishing High Risk

Text messages masquerading as courier services, banks, or utilities asking you to tap a short bit.ly link.

Clue: Urgent warnings arriving from normal 10-digit mobile numbers.
3. Voice / Vishing Critical

Phone calls from impersonators pretending to be police, tax authorities, bank managers, or tech support.

Clue: Coercing you into installing AnyDesk or keeping strict secrecy.
4. Social Media Scams High Risk

Hacked friend accounts, fake copyright strike warnings, and fraudulent giveaway contests in direct messages.

Clue: Friends urgently requesting funds without voice confirmation.
5. Fake Websites Critical

Identical clones of banking, shopping, and airline portals designed to steal credentials and card details.

Clue: Deceptive subdomains like `bank.com.fake-login.xyz`.
6. QR-Code Scams (Quishing) High Risk

Malicious QR stickers pasted over legitimate merchant codes, parking meters, or 'Scan to Receive Money' tricks.

Clue: Remember you NEVER enter your PIN to receive funds!

Scam Messages & Bank KYC Process

Detailed breakdown of the 5-stage Bank KYC scam lifecycle, real scam message samples, and official banking defense rules.

Bank KYC Scam Process (5 Attack Stages)

How criminals execute unauthorized fund siphoning under the guise of PAN/KYC updates

HIGH SEVERITY
STAGE 01

The Panic Lure

Mass SMS alert: “Account BLOCKED today due to incomplete PAN KYC. Click link immediately.”

Clue: 24h urgency & 10-digit phone sender.
STAGE 02

Fake Link or APK

Link opens cloned banking page or downloads rogue file (e.g. SBI_KYC_Verif.apk).

Clue: .xyz/.online domain, asks for card PIN.
STAGE 03

Screen Sharing Trap

Fraudster calls claiming to be bank manager; asks you to install AnyDesk or QuickSupport.

Clue: Bank staff NEVER asks to share screen.
STAGE 04

OTP Interception

Malicious APK or screen share intercepts incoming bank SMS containing transaction OTPs.

Clue: OTP explicitly states transfer amount!
STAGE 05

Account Draining

Funds are moved within 180s into money mule accounts and immediately cashed out.

Defense: Dial 1930 within the Golden Hour.
Official Central Bank (RBI / FTC) Advisory on Re-KYC:

Legitimate banks NEVER ask for KYC updates over SMS links, WhatsApp, or phone calls. Re-KYC is completed strictly in-person at your branch or through the authenticated mobile app downloaded from Google Play or Apple App Store. Never share an OTP or install AnyDesk.

1. Fake Bank & KYC Messages

Severe
“Dear Customer, your HDFC Bank account will be SUSPENDED within 24 hours due to non-updated PAN card KYC. Click here immediately: http://hdfc-kyc-verify.online/pan”

How it works: The link mimics your bank portal. Typing your customer ID and OTP hands over control to drain your savings.

✓ Safe action: Never click links in SMS. Open your official bank app directly.

2. Fake Job Offers

High Risk
“Earn ₹3,000–₹8,000 daily by just liking YouTube videos & rating hotels on Google Maps. No experience needed. Join Telegram VIP task manager.”

How it works: They pay small initial amounts ($5), then demand $500 “security deposit” to unlock fake $1,500 earnings.

✓ Safe action: Any job that requires YOU to deposit money is 100% a scam.

3. Shopping Scams

High Risk
“MEGA FLASH SALE! Brand new Nike Air Jordans and PS5 consoles at 90% discount! Only next 2 hours at nike-superclearance.shop!”

How it works: Fake social ads harvest credit card details or non-refundable payments; items never arrive.

✓ Safe action: If a discount seems too good to be true, it is always a scam.

4. Lottery & Prize Scams

Severe
“CONGRATULATIONS! Your number won the ₹25 Lakh KBC WhatsApp Lucky Draw! Contact Manager Rana Pratap to claim after paying ₹15,000 GST fee.”

How it works: Victims pay endless clearance and tax fees for a prize that does not exist.

✓ Safe action: You cannot win a lottery you never entered or bought a ticket for.

5. UPI & Payment Scams

Severe
“I am ready to buy your furniture on OLX. Scan this QR code and type your 6-digit UPI PIN to receive ₹15,000 into your Google Pay immediately.”

How it works: Entering your UPI PIN authorises money LEAVING your account, never receiving it!

✓ Safe action: Golden Rule: Never enter UPI PIN or scan a QR code to receive money.

6. Fake Customer Care Numbers

Severe
Google search result ad: “Swiggy / Amazon 24x7 Customer Helpline: Dial +91-70020-XXXXX for instant refund of stuck order.”

How it works: Scammers put their own mobile numbers on Google ads and instruct callers to install AnyDesk.

✓ Safe action: Always access customer support from inside the authenticated app.

7. Social Media Account Scams

High Risk
Instagram DM: “Notice: Copyright Infringement detected on your account. Your profile will be deleted in 24 hours. Submit appeal form: https://meta-appeal-badge-help.com”

How it works: The fake page steals your Instagram password and 2FA code, locking you out.

✓ Safe action: Meta and Instagram never send copyright or policy strikes via direct messages.

MODULE 04 // 7 RULES

How to Stay Safe: The 7 Golden Rules

Essential digital defense habits to protect your banking, data, and family.

RULE #1

Don't click suspicious links

Never tap shortened or unexpected links in text messages. Type the official URL manually.

RULE #2

Verify the sender

Expand the full sender email domain. Display names like “Netflix Support” can easily be forged.

RULE #3

Never share OTP / PIN / passwords

Your OTP is strictly for transactions YOU initiated. Legitimate bank staff will NEVER ask for it.

RULE #4

Check website addresses carefully

Look for typosquatting (e.g. `paypa1.com` or `chase-security.online`). Inspect the actual domain.

RULE #5

Use strong passwords

Use 14+ character passphrases combining 3-4 random words, symbols, and numbers. Use a password manager.

RULE #6

Enable two-factor authentication (2FA)

Turn on 2FA using Google or Microsoft Authenticator apps so stolen passwords cannot unlock your accounts.

RULE #7

Keep apps and devices updated

Enable automatic updates on Android, iOS, Windows, and Mac. Updates patch the security vulnerabilities exploited by hackers.

EMERGENCY INCIDENT PROTOCOL

Report & Help Channels

What to do after encountering a suspected scam and official country reporting numbers.

STEP 01

Freeze Cards & Accounts

Call your bank immediately or toggle “Lock Card” in your mobile app to prevent further debits.

STEP 02

Call National Fraud Helpline

In India dial 1930 (within 1-2 hours) to trigger an inter-bank lien freezing the recipient account.

STEP 03

Change Passwords & 2FA

Reset master passwords for email and banking. Revoke all active login sessions.
